\subsection*{Entry Instructions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Entry Instructions}

\begin{itemize}
\item Carbonation, sweetness, and strength levels must be specified for all styles. Minor differences from stated levels should not be heavily penalized or used as justification for disqualification.
\item Perceived carbonation is dependent on judging conditions, so specifying or requiring absolute volumes of CO2 is not necessary.
\item Declaring honey varietals is allowable in any style. Further information about the season or source may be provided. Judges may assume wildflower (unknown or polyfloral) honey if no information is furnished.
\item Fermenting or aging in wood is allowable in any style, but must be declared if noticeable. Judges will treat this as a background character unless in a Specialty Mead style (M4D, M4E, M4F).
\item Several styles require additional information, typically about added ingredients. Additional explanatory information may be supplied if the ingredient or process is not well-known to judges. Comments should be limited to explanations, not judging conclusions.
\item Potential allergens must always be declared, but do not need to be perceived by judges. For this purpose, do not declare ingredients that are either required by the style (and thus assumed to be present) or already declared (do not be redundant).
\end{itemize}

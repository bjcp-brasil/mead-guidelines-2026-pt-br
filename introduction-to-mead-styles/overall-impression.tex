\subsection*{Overall Impression}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Overall Impression}

\begin{itemize}
\item Mead should express an enjoyable balance of honey flavors, sweetness, acidity, tannins, and alcohol.
\item Any additional ingredients should likewise be well-integrated into the final presentation, not seem like an add-on or afterthought.
\item Mead should be well-fermented and conditioned, presenting as a coherent fermented beverage not a raw, honey-based cocktail.
\item Flavors should be a pure, fresh, and natural expression of the declared ingredients. Fermentation and conditioning can add complexity.
\end{itemize}

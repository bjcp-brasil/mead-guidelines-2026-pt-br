\section*{M1. Traditional Mead}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{M1. Traditional Mead}
\textit{Traditional Meads are fermented from honey and water with no additional adjuncts or additives, other than common flavor-neutral processing aids. Aging in traditional wood is allowable in any style as a relatively neutral, background character. The styles within this category are divided by perceived residual sweetness, which allows for less fatiguing judging.}
